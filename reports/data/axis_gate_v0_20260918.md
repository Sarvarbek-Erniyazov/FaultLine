**It is not a CARE label category.** CARE's labels come from event_info, the single fault label `anomaly` (`configs/data/events_v2.yaml`, `event_info`), so the variant removes **0 of 45 CARE events and touches no farm**: on `care__test` the two label columns are identical, and the variant loses 0 of CARE's 430,506 windows and 0 of its 540 positives. The variant therefore exists for the temporal axis alone, which is where `configs/eval/axis_gate_v0.yaml` puts it.
