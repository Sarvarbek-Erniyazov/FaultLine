## How an absent channel is imposed

The CARE adapter never writes `<nan>` itself. A farm's absent core channel is a column its frame does not carry. `QuantileBinTokenizer.transform` emits `MISSING_BIN` for it on every row, and `JointVocab.encode_steps` maps that to `<nan>` (id **9**, the shard manifest's) at the channel's fixed position in the step, `1 + channel_index`, after `<sep>`. The stream carries no channel tokens: position in the step is the channel. F6-0a overwrites exactly those positions of every step of every window with that id, at inference. Nothing else changes: `<sep>`, the other channels and the window boundaries are the unmasked read's.

Checked before scoring: masking real CARE windows with their own farm's pattern leaves every one of them unchanged (farm_a: 256 windows, farm_b: 256 windows, farm_c: 256 windows). The unmasked first batch, re-scored here, reproduces F3's saved logits (largest absolute difference: S2_trained_seed1 0.0e+00, S2_trained_seed2 0.0e+00, S2_trained_seed3 0.0e+00).
