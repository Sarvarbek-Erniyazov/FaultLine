# M2 Gate 6: text tokenizer and text-only pretraining

**Date:** 2026-09-16. **Scope:** the PHMSA gate ruling and its execution, M2c (BPE fit),
M2d (shards, pretraining) and M2e (per-source evaluation). Phase B / M3 is not started.

**Headline.** The M2 pipeline is complete end to end and every done-when criterion is
met. The corpus is 8.05M whitespace words, **26.8% of the 30M floor**, from two agencies.
The tokenizer is fitted once at 32,768. Both text rungs trained to their pre-registered
budget and **neither is a result about scale**: the larger rung is slightly worse, and
both are undertrained by construction (one pass over 10.0M tokens).

### 1a. The robots.txt stop condition (step 0)

`urllib.robotparser` ends a group at a blank line, silently dropping `Disallow` lines
written below one. **On `www.nrc.gov`, the host actually crawled, this did not happen:**
its `user-agent:*` group is lines 1-6 with no blank line inside it, and the first blank
line only closes the group.

A different defect does apply there. nrc.gov writes its rule values as full URLs
(`Disallow: https://www.nrc.gov/site-help/search`), which the stdlib parser stores
percent-encoded and can never match against a path, so all four `*` rules read as
allow-all. Cross-checked against the staged manifests: every nrc.gov URL staged is under
`/reading-rm/` or `/documents-reports/`, and **no staged document came from
`/ads.txt/`, `/autodiscover/` or `/site-help/search`.** Nothing had to be unstaged.

`download/robots.py` replaces the stdlib parser: blank lines are not group boundaries,
a full-URL value naming the same host is read as its path (one naming another host is
dropped), `*` and `$` work, longest match wins, `Allow` wins ties, and `Crawl-delay` is
honoured when longer than the client's own interval. Both real files are captured byte
for byte as fixtures; the regression test asserts `/api/odata/x` and `/login` are refused
on `data.transportation.gov`.

## 2. PHMSA: the rule and its outcome

The rule was committed (`7542165`) **before** the file was fetched or read (`4257351`).

Measured on the target attachment (`reports/data/phmsa_manual_20260915.md`): one tabular
member, tab-delimited Windows-1252, 648 columns, all 5,850 data lines read. 62 columns
qualify, all by name. `NARRATIVE`: 5,820 non-null values, mean 953 characters,
distinct-value ratio 1.000, **894,467 whitespace tokens**; all qualifying columns
**1,030,940**. Both clear the 250,000 bar, so the rule's third branch applied: stage all
2010-onward files.

**Note on the threshold, which did not change the outcome.** 61 of the 62 qualifying
columns are short `*_DETAILS` "other, specify" fields, some of them closed code sets
(`CAUSE_DETAILS`: 5,850 values, distinct ratio 0.007). A future rule of this shape should
apply the distinct-ratio guard to name matches too. `NARRATIVE` alone is 3.6x the bar, so
the branch is the same under any reading. Only `NARRATIVE` is staged (a staging choice,
stated as such).

Staged: **10,033 documents** across six files. Each zip was fetched through the
robots-gated client, parsed, and only then recorded in the `phmsa` manifest.

## 4. M2c: the tokenizer (`reports/data/text_bpe_v1_20260915.md`)

One fit, no sweep. **32,512 merges, 32,768 ids**, filling ADR-0003's text region exactly,
fitted on the train split only (31,750 documents, 10,012,124 tokens).

| source | val bytes/token | test bytes/token |
| --- | --- | --- |
| nrc_event_notifications | 4.752 | 4.795 |
| nrc_gen_letters | 4.802 | 4.815 |

**Limitation, reported not fixed:** 24,456 of 32,768 ids (**74.63%**) are seen fewer than
100 times in training, and 931 never appear in the final training encoding (intermediate
merges absorbed by later ones). A 32,768 vocabulary is large for a 10M-token corpus. The
brief pre-registers one fit, so this is a stated limitation, not a refit trigger.

**ADR-0007 readiness:** the 264 status strings encode to 5.47 tokens on average (2-20).

*Correction, 2026-09-16 (after Gate 6).* The rare share above is wrong in the report that
produced it: `render_report` counted over a `Counter` of the ids that occurred, so the 931
ids never seen were not counted as seen fewer than 100 times. The vocabulary holds
**25,387 such ids, 77.47%**, recounted from the training shards; the counter is fixed and
tested (`tests/data/text/test_bpe_fit.py::test_rare_id_count_includes_ids_never_seen`).
The distribution of tokens per status string, per site, and its token- and word-level
coverage are on the code book's card, `data/cards/status_code_book.md`.

## 6. M2d/M2e: pretraining (`reports/data/text_pretrain_v1_20260916.md`)

Budget per rung: one pass over the training tokens (4,905 windows) or 4 GPU-hours,
whichever is smaller. Both rungs hit **one_pass** and spent 4,896 windows (153 steps of
16x2). S2 101.8 min, S3 120.1 min on one RTX 4060.

| rung | parameters | validation loss (nats) | train loss |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S2 | 10,227,072 | **6.1474** | 5.8819 |
| S3 | 19,986,624 | 6.1977 | 5.9299 |

**The larger rung is not better, and this is not a scaling result.** Both rungs were
still improving at every measurement to the last one, so both are undertrained: one pass
over 10.0M tokens is the pre-registered budget, not a sufficient one. Two undertrained
points at one seed each cannot separate "S3 is worse" from "S3 needs more than one pass";
no claim is made in either direction.

*Corrected 2026-09-16 (`docs/ROADMAP.md`, M2).* Both rungs spent the same 10,027,008
tokens, so neither a token-count nor a degree-of-undertraining explanation separates them,
and "S3 needs more than one pass" is withdrawn as a reading of the ordering. At 0.98 (S2)
and 0.50 (S3) tokens per parameter, both rungs are far left of any size-token balance
point, where a larger model is less sample-efficient. The ordering carries no scaling
information, and a ladder over a corpus of about 10M tokens cannot demonstrate scaling.

## 9. Defects found and fixed while running this gate

Five, all with tests, each found by reading code before trusting its output:

1. `inspect phmsa` wrote a `verified=True` manifest record **before** opening the zip
   (`ebfa946`). The first real inspection exited 2 and recorded nothing, as intended: the
   member is tab-delimited cp1252, which the comma/UTF-8 reader could not parse.
2. The robots gate (section 1a), `ebfa946`.
3. A text shard split shorter than one context wrote an empty window index with a warning;
   it now raises (`ebfa946`).
4. `text_ladder` counted "one pass" as the stride-1 admissible-window count, 10,031,592
   windows -- about 2,000 passes -- so the GPU-hour cap would always have bound; and
   bits per byte multiplied by one window's tokens while dividing by a whole split's
   bytes (`a9ce65e`). Both definitions are now pinned by tests.
5. The throughput calibration timed a full validation measurement as if it were training
   (`bc0fd3a`): S2 read 0.7 windows/s instead of 5.7. Left unfixed, S3 would have
   measured ~0.24 windows/s and been cut to ~3,400 windows by a spurious GPU-hour bound,
   making the two rungs non-comparable. Found at S2's calibration line; the run was
   stopped and restarted rather than reported.

Two further fixes: `text_ladder` never saved the selected parameters (`16767ab`), which
M2's done-when requires and M3's ablation arm starts from; and the BPE fit's full-recount
loop was replaced by an incremental one (`3cbcb2d`, identical merges asserted against the
original loop on real text past its first tie, and on tie-heavy inputs).

## 10. Limitations carried into M3

- **26.8% of the 30M floor**, two agencies, one language, neither about wind turbines.
- **77.47% of the tokenizer's vocabulary is rare** at this corpus size (74.63% as first
  reported, before the never-seen ids were counted; correction in section 4).
- **Both rungs are undertrained** at one pass; no scaling or architecture claim.
- *Consolidated 2026-09-16:* the 77.47% rare share, the no-scaling ladder, and a 32,768-id
  text region too large for this corpus are **one** limitation, not three. The configured
  capacities are sized for a corpus several times larger than the licence-clean route
  produced (`docs/ROADMAP.md`, M2).
- *2026-09-16:* **H3 is withdrawn on evidence** (ADR-0007); the wind vocabulary gap below is
  now a recorded negative result, not a pending test.
- **`nrc_reg_issues` is a 53-document source** with more held-out than training words.
- **The wind-specific vocabulary gap is unclosed** (section 7), and it is the gap H3's
  event-type split depends on.
- Evaluation samples up to 3,000 overlapping stride-1 windows per source per measurement,
  which is why scoring dominated wall clock (S3: 120 min training, ~2 h scoring).
- M1's own status is unchanged and still governs what may be claimed: **H1 UNTESTED, the
  pretraining ablation INCONCLUSIVE**.
