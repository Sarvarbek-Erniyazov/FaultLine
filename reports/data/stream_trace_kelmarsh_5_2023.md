## 6. What this is not

This trace adds no rule, no ADR and no verdict, and changes nothing in the record. It is one turbine-year of one site, chosen for holding the most events, and the site is a training site: the split is forward in time, not across sites. Its AUPRC is therefore not comparable with the pooled gate numbers, and the programme's findings (ADR-0025 INCONCLUSIVE, ADR-0026's read-out result) stand exactly as the ledger records them.
